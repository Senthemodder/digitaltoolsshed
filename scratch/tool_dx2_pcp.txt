      "a": "A downhole Progressing Cavity Pump (PCP) is a positive displacement artificial lift system consisting of a single-lead helical metallic rotor turning inside a double-lead internal helical elastomeric stator. As the rotor turns eccentrically, continuously sealed cavities form and progress from the suction intake to the discharge, lifting viscous fluids without pulsating. PCPs are the industry standard for producing heavy crude oil (8° to 22° API), high-water-cut emulsions, and abrasive sand-laden slurries where electrical submersible pumps (ESPs) and reciprocating rod pumps suffer rapid mechanical failure."

If the interference fit is too loose, fluid slips backwards across the seal lines under high differential pressure, causing volumetric efficiency to collapse from 85% to under 30%. Conversely, if the fit is too tight (due to thermal expansion or aromatic crude swelling), friction torque spikes, generating destructive hysteretic internal heating that blisters and melts the elastomer."
