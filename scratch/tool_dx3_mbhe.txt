      "a": "A Moving Bed Heat Exchanger (MBHE) is an indirect conduction cooler for free-flowing bulk granular materials (fertilizer prills, plastic pellets, grains, sugar, detergents, and sand). Bulk solids flow slowly downward by gravity in dense plug flow between banks of vertical, parallel stainless-steel pillow plates. Cooling water or thermal oil circulates internally within the plates in counter-current flow. Because solids never contact cooling air directly, the system eliminates dust emissions, air scrubbers, cyclones, and high-energy fluidizing blowers."
