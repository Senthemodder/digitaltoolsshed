Steel and HDPE pipes without internal ballast are significantly lighter than the displaced slurry, creating substantial positive buoyancy. This upward buoyant force presses the crown of the pipe tightly against the upper roof of the borehole, tripling normal friction. Filling the pipe with ballast water during pullback neutralizes buoyancy, reducing pulling tension by up to 50% to 70%."

Even a modest friction coefficient (μ = 0.30) across an aggregated bend angle of 20° to 30° multiplies pulling tension significantly. Keeping bore bend radii generous (typically R > 1200 × OD for steel, or R > 40–50 × OD for HDPE) minimizes this exponential tension escalation."
